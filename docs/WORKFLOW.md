# How the CRM works: a step-by-step guide

This guide follows a school from the first phone call to the money in the bank, and explains every screen and
button on the way. It is written for everyone at Wonder Learning: salespeople, managers, accounts and the owner.

> **The one-line version:** a **lead** (a school we're talking to) becomes an **opportunity** (a deal we're
> working on), which becomes a **client** (a school that signed). For a client we take a **purchase order (PO)**,
> make a **sales order**, send the **kits**, raise the **invoice** and record the **payments**. The CRM books the
> follow-up calls for you at every step.

## 1. The journey at a glance

| # | Stage | Where | What you do | What the CRM does by itself |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead** | Leads | Add the school (or upload many from Excel) | Gives it a number (L-1001…), books the first follow-up call |
| 2 | **Talk** | Lead page | Call / WhatsApp, then **Log interaction** | Status moves New → Contacted; books the next follow-up if you give a date |
| 3 | **Quotation** (optional at this stage) | Lead page → Quotations | **Create quotation**, then **Send quotation** | Numbers it (QUO/2026/10/001), marks the lead Contacted |
| 4 | **Opportunity** | Lead page | **Convert to opportunity**, pick Hot or Warm | Creates the deal (O-2001…); the lead's quotations and follow-ups move with it |
| 5 | **Work the deal** | Opportunities / Pipeline | Move it through the stages; send / revise quotations | Sets the chance % per stage; books follow-ups |
| 6 | **PO template** | Quotation → **Send PO template** | Type the kits per class and order details, **Save**, send on WhatsApp | Gives a PO number (PO/2627/1), attaches the kit checklist, fills the deal value |
| 7 | **Won** | Opportunity → Stage: Won | Mark Won, then **Convert to client** | Creates the client (C-101…) in Onboarding with an onboarding follow-up |
| 8 | **Signed PO → sales order** | Client → **Upload signed PO** | Upload the school's signed PO | Creates the sales order (SO/…) with the kits and PO number |
| 9 | **Kits** | Sales order → **Dispatch kits** | Send kits in one or more lots | Makes the delivery challan; you mark it received |
| 10 | **Invoice** | Sales order → **Create invoice** | Raise the invoice (due in 45 days by default) | Numbers it (INV/…); shows it on Outstanding |
| 11 | **Payments** | Invoice → **Record payment** | Record each instalment (cash, UPI, NEFT, cheque…) | Sends it to **Accounts for approval**; books the next collection call on the promised date |
| 11a | **Approval** | Accounts → **Payment approvals** | Accounts checks the bank and presses **Approve** (or **Reject** with a reason) | Approved: counts as received, gets the receipt number (117/26-27), the receipt can be sent; the salesperson gets a "Send receipt" to-do |
| 12 | **Next year** | Clients → **Renewal** | Create renewal opportunities for next financial year | Values them at this year's orders; books a call |

Nothing is ever deleted at year end. A school that doesn't renew stays in Clients with its full history.

## 2. Signing in and choosing the year

1. Open the CRM (or the Android app) and sign in with your email and password.
   - 5 wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. **Forgot password?** sends a reset link once email is set
     up; until then an Admin can set a new password in Settings → Users.
2. **Choose the financial year** (April – March), shown as **2026-27**, or **All years**.
   - Lists, the dashboard and downloads follow the year you choose. A school's own page always shows everything.
   - The year shows at the top (**FY 2026-27**; on phones **26-27**). Tap it to switch any time.
   - "Following the year" means *alive during that year*: leads and deals added that year **plus older ones
     still open**, and invoices of that year **plus older ones still unpaid**. So nothing open is lost on 1 April.

## 3. Finding your way around

**Top bar (every screen)**

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **FY 2026-27** | Change the financial year |
| **Search** (box on a computer, magnifier on a phone) | Find any school, lead, client, invoice or quotation by name, number or mobile |
| **+ Add** | New lead · New to-do · Open the pipeline · Find a client |
| **Bell** | What needs attention now: overdue to-dos, to-dos due today, overdue invoices, cheques in hand not yet deposited, clients due for renewal. Tap one to open it |

**Menu (left side; on a phone tap ☰)**: Dashboard · Leads · Opportunities · Pipeline · To-do · Clients ·
Outstanding · Ledger · How it works (this guide), and for Admins: Settings (Users, Products, Locations, Documents,
Targets, Features, Project documents). At the bottom: **Download app**, change password (key icon), **Sign out**.

**On every list:** **PDF** and **Excel** download exactly what you see (same filters, same year).
**Phones:** the page scrolls up and down only; pop-up windows open from the bottom.

## 4. Who sees what

| Role | Sees | Can also |
|---|---|---|
| **Director**, **Admin** | Everyone's leads, deals, clients, money | All settings, users, products, money corrections |
| **Sales Head** | Everyone's | Products & prices, targets, money corrections, give work to anyone in sales |
| **Sales Manager**, **Sales Executive** | **Only their own** leads, deals, clients and to-dos | — |

"**Assigned to**" always means the salesperson responsible. "**Owner**" means the school's owner.
**Money corrections** (cancel an invoice, delete a payment, credit notes, cheque cleared / bounced) are only for
Director, Admin and Sales Head.

## 5. Leads

### Adding a lead
- **+ Add → New lead**, or Leads → **New lead**.
- Required (*): School name, Owner / contact person, Mobile number, State, City, Lead source, Assigned to, Next
  follow-up date. Lead source includes **Social Media** (Facebook, Instagram etc.); "Reference name" appears for
  reference sources.
- If the same mobile, or the same school name in the same city, already exists, a **possible duplicate** warning
  shows who has it. You can still save if it really is a different school.
- Choosing status **Qualified** turns it straight into an opportunity (you'll be asked Hot or Warm).
- **After saving:** the first follow-up call is booked in To-do on the follow-up date.

### Many leads at once (Excel)
1. Leads → **Download template** (always download a fresh one; an old template is refused).
2. Fill one school per row. Drop-downs show the allowed values; row 2 is an example and is skipped.
3. Leads → **Upload leads**. The CRM checks every row like the form does and lists what was added and what was
   skipped, with the reason.

### The lead list
Filters: **All · Active · Converted · Disqualified**, source, search, sort, **Added from – to** dates.
**Convert to opportunity** is right on the row.

### The lead page
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Call** / **WhatsApp** | Calls or opens WhatsApp to the contact's mobile |
| **Log interaction** | Write what was discussed (call, WhatsApp, meeting, visit…). A follow-up date creates a to-do. A New lead becomes Contacted. You can tick "Convert to opportunity" at the same time |
| **Convert to opportunity** | Creates the deal; choose **Hot** or **Warm** (the category belongs to the deal; it can be changed to Cold later in the opportunity window if the deal cools down) |
| **Disqualify** | Not interested / not a fit: choose the reason. Open to-dos are cancelled |
| **Create quotation** | Quotation before converting (some schools ask straight away). See §6 |
| **Save changes** / **Discard changes** | Edit any detail; the buttons wake up when you change a field |
| **Open opportunity** | (after converting) jumps to the deal |
| **Edit school details** | (after converting or disqualifying) correct a wrong name, contact, mobile, email or address |

**One school, one set of details.** A correction made on the lead, in the opportunity window (**Edit school
details**) or on the client (**Edit details**) is saved on all three, and on draft quotations, so every screen,
list, invoice and receipt shows the same details. Quotations already sent keep what the school received.

Lead statuses: **New → Contacted → Qualified**, or **Disqualified**; **Converted** once it's an opportunity.

## 6. Quotations

You can quote on a **lead**, an **opportunity** or a **client** (for repeat orders). The **Quotations** card has the
same buttons everywhere.

### Making one: **Create quotation**
1. Check **To**, **Date**, **Validity (days)**, **School name**, **Address**.
2. **Choose a product to add… → Add.** MRP and price fill in from Settings → Products (you can change them).
3. For each line:
   - **Description**: what the school sees (editable).
   - **MRP (₹)** and **Price (₹)** per kit.
   - **Transport / kit (hidden)**: transport for this school's location, per kit. It is **added into the price**
     and **never printed** anywhere: the school only sees the total ("School sees ₹2,934 per kit"). Put it on
     whichever line(s) you choose.
   - **Change kit items** (kits only): **untick** items to take them out (price and MRP go down by that item's
     price) and **add optional items** (Bag, Diary, Hindi books…; price goes up). The description updates by
     itself, e.g. "Nursery Focus Kit (without Shape Kit; with Bag)".
   - **Remove**: drops the line.
4. **Create draft.** Drafts can be **Edit**ed or **Delete**d.

### Sending it: **Send quotation**
- **Preview PDF**, **Download PDF** (to attach yourself), **Share on WhatsApp** (a private link to the PDF), or
  **Email from the CRM** (once email is set up).
- Sending **locks** it and marks it **Sent**. On an early deal it moves the stage to **Proposal Sent**; on a lead it
  marks the lead Contacted. A follow-up is booked for the day before it expires.

### After sending
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **View PDF** | Open the quotation |
| **Send again** | Share the same quotation again |
| **Revise** | A new draft copied from it (new number) to change prices or kits |
| **Send PO template** | See §8 |

After its validity date a sent quotation shows **Expired** (feature switch "Quotation validity").

## 7. Opportunities and the pipeline

- **Opportunities** is the list (filters: stage, category Hot/Warm/Cold, salesperson, **Added from – to**);
  **Board view** opens the **Pipeline**, which shows the stages side by side (one under another on a phone).
- Drag a card to another stage, or open it and change **Stage**.

**Stages and chance:** Interested 20% → Demo Scheduled 35% → Proposal Sent 55% → Negotiation 75% → **Won** 100% /
**Lost** 0%. A lead that already received a quotation starts at **Proposal Sent**.

**The opportunity window**
- **Stage**, **Category**, **Expected deal value (₹)**, **Expected close date**, **Competitor**, **Next action** and
  date, **Decision maker**, **Assigned to**. Change any, then **Save changes**.
- **Expected deal value** fills in by itself from the PO template (kits × rate) if nobody has typed one. Pipeline
  totals and the dashboard use it.
- **Log interaction**, **Edit school details** (correct name, contact, mobile, address: saved on the lead and
  client too), the **Quotations** card, open follow-ups and **Stage history** are in the same window.
- Moving to **Proposal Sent** or **Negotiation** books a follow-up in 2 days.
- **Lost** asks for the reason and remarks (and competitor). Open follow-ups are cancelled. Won and Lost are final.
- **Won** → the window shows **Convert to client**.

## 8. The PO template (purchase order for the school to sign)

On a **sent** quotation: **Send PO template**.
1. **Quantities (kits demanded)**: number of kits per line (or leave blank for the school to fill in).
2. **Order details**: requisitioner, expected delivery date, ship via, shipping terms, remarks.
3. **Customised with school name & logo**: YES / NO per item.
4. **Advance cheque / DD details**: up to 4 cheques (CDC, PDC, DD, NEFT) with dates and amounts.
5. **Save**: the CRM gives the **PO number** (PO/2627/1, running through the financial year) and fills the deal
   value. Then **Preview**, **Download** or **WhatsApp** it to the school.

**What the school receives:** the PO in your format with their name, kits, rates (transport already inside),
terms, bank details and signature boxes. The **Material Exclude** box lists any items taken out of a kit (e.g.
"Nursery Focus Kit: Shape Kit"), or "Nothing excluded". **The kit checklist is attached from page 2**: every item in
each kit, with removed items left out and added items listed under "Added for this school". The WhatsApp message
also carries a separate checklist link.

## 9. Clients

**Convert to client** (on a Won deal) creates the client in **Onboarding** with a "Start onboarding" follow-up.
When set-up is finished, press **Onboarding complete** → **Active**.

### The Clients list
- **All · Onboarding · Active**, search, **Client since from – to**.
- **In 2026-27** (the chosen year): **Renewed** (ordered this year and last), **New** (first order ever), **Ordered
  again** (back after a gap), **Not renewed** (ordered last year, not this year), **No order**, with this year's
  order value. Use **Not renewed** to call schools that haven't come back. With **All years** the column shows the
  last year each school ordered.
- **Renewals for FY 2027-28 → Create renewals**: one renewal opportunity per client who ordered (Admin / Sales Head).

### The client page
**Header:** % collected ring, status, client number, salesperson. Buttons: **Edit details** (address, contact,
salesperson; corrections also update the lead and opportunity), **Renewal FY 2027-28** (this client only), **Call**, **WhatsApp**, **Log interaction**,
**Onboarding complete**.
**Strip:** Quotations → Sales orders → Invoiced → Received → Outstanding → Overdue.
**Left:** About this school, open follow-ups, **Contacts** (**Add contact**: Owner, Principal, Accounts…; mark
who gets payment messages).
**Tabs:**

**Quotations & orders**
- Quotations card (as §6).
- **Purchase order (PO):** step 1 **Send PO template**; step 2 **Upload signed PO**.
- **Upload signed PO** / **New sales order** → **From quotation …** (kits and PO number already filled from the PO
  template) or **Blank order**. Check kits, price, expected delivery, the school's PO number and date, attach the
  signed PO (PDF or photo, up to 4 MB) → **Create order**.
- On each sales order:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **View signed PO** / **PO** | See or add the school's PO |
| **Take advance** | Money received before invoicing; gets a receipt and comes off the invoice later |
| **Proforma invoice** | Printable proforma (PI/…) |
| **Dispatch kits** | Send a lot: date, kits per line, transporter, docket / LR, vehicle → **Challan PDF** to go with the kits |
| **Mark received** / **Upload proof** | When the school gets the kits; attach proof of delivery |
| **Mark delivered** | The whole order is delivered |
| **Create invoice** | Raise the invoice for the order |
| Cancel | Cancel the order (cancel its invoice first; an advance must be removed by an Admin) |

**Invoices & payments**

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **View PDF** | The invoice |
| **Record payment** | Amount, date, mode (NEFT/RTGS, UPI, Cheque, CDC, PDC, Cash, Other), reference. **Part payment** asks for the **next promised date** → a collection follow-up on that date. **Cheques** start **In hand** and get a "Deposit cheque" reminder on the cheque date |
| **Send reminder** | WhatsApp or email reminder with a link to the invoice |
| **Send invoice** | Share the invoice on WhatsApp / email |
| **Credit note** | Reduce the invoice (return, discount, write-off) — finance roles |
| Cancel invoice | Finance roles; the order can then be invoiced again |

Payments received: a payment recorded by a salesperson first shows **Awaiting approval** (no receipt yet) until
Accounts approves it; a rejected one shows **Rejected** with the reason. After approval: **Receipt** (Preview,
Download, WhatsApp, email), cheque buttons **Deposited → Cleared** or **Bounced** (bounced = amount due again, with an
urgent follow-up). **Delete**: finance roles, or the person who recorded it while it waits or after a rejection.
Money counts as **received** only when Accounts has approved it **and** it is Received or Cleared.

### Accounts: payment approvals
For now this section is in the **Admin / Director** login (menu **Accounts → Payment approvals**, with a count of
what's waiting; the bell shows it too). Each payment shows the school, invoice or order, amount, date, mode,
reference / cheque details and who recorded it.
- **Approve**: it counts as received, gets the next receipt number, and the receipt window opens to send it. The
  person who recorded it gets a "Send receipt" to-do (it closes when the receipt is shared).
- **Reject**: write the reason (e.g. "UTR not found in bank"). It never counts and gets no receipt; the person who
  recorded it gets a to-do with the reason, so they can check with the school and record it again.
- **Decided in the last 30 days** lists approvals (with **Send receipt**) and rejections. **PDF / Excel** download.
- Payments recorded by Accounts themselves count straight away. Switch the approval step off in Settings →
  Features → "Payment approval by Accounts".

**Documents:** **Upload document** (agreement, GST certificate…). **Timeline:** everything that happened.

## 10. To-do

- Opens on the **Calendar**: **Month** or **Week** (hours 8 AM – 8 PM), **Today**, **‹ ›**. Blue = school
  follow-up, purple = own to-do, red = overdue, grey = done.
- **Double-click a day** (or an hour in Week) to add a to-do there. On a phone: tap the day, then **+ Add**.
- Tap an item → **Done** (write the outcome, optionally book the next one), **Postpone** (new date + reason;
  postponements are counted), **Cancel to-do**.
- **List** view: Overdue / Today / Upcoming, with the same buttons and a **Due from – to** filter.
- **New to-do**: your own work (Internal Meeting, Document Preparation, Report / Admin Work, Training, Reminder,
  Other) or a school follow-up; optional time and priority. Managers can show the whole team.

**Follow-ups the CRM books by itself:** new lead (first call) · interaction with a follow-up date · conversion
(schedule demo, or follow up the quotation) · Proposal Sent / Negotiation (in 2 days) · quotation about to expire ·
new client (start onboarding) · part payment (collect on the promised date) · cheque date (deposit cheque) ·
bounced cheque · renewal (call in 7 days).

## 11. Outstanding and Ledger

- **Outstanding:** what schools still owe. Totals, **How late is the money?** (not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90,
  90+ days), **Expected collections** (late, this week, this month, later), and each invoice with **Record
  payment** and **Send reminder**. Show: **To collect · Overdue · Paid · All**; **Invoice date from – to**.
- **Ledger:** invoices (debit) and payments / credit notes (credit) with opening and closing balance. **This
  financial year · Last financial year · This month · Custom dates**; all clients or pick one for its statement.

## 11a. Expenses and advances

**Everyone: My expenses** (menu). For hotel, travel, meals and other work spends:
1. **Add expense** → **Is the bill available?** **Yes** → **Take photo / choose file** (the phone camera opens; big
   photos are shrunk automatically; up to 4 bills, photo or PDF). **No bill** → describe the expense and why there is
   no bill. Then date, amount, kind (Travel, Local travel / fuel, Hotel / stay, Meals, Courier,
   Printing, Phone, School meeting, Services, Software / licences, Marketing, Office, Other), **who paid**
   (**Own money** = pay me back, **From my advance**, or **Company credit card**), city, what it was for, optionally the school → **Save**.
2. It goes for approval to the **Director** (Admin approves until a Director login is created). The page shows **Advance with me**, **To be paid back to me**, **Waiting
   for approval** and **Approved this year**, each expense with its status and bill, and the advances received.
3. **Delete** your expense while it waits or after a rejection (then add it again correctly).

**Accounts → Expenses** (Director / Admin for now):
- The page says who approves. **Waiting for approval**: open the **Bill** (or read the "No bill" description), then
  **Approve** or **Reject** (reason; the person gets a to-do). Approve / Reject buttons appear only for the
  Director (or Admin while there is no Director login); Admin's own expenses also go to the Director.
- **People**: per employee, advance with them (given − returned − spent from advance), what to pay back, what's
  waiting. **Pay back ₹…** marks all their approved own-money spends as paid (date + UTR / voucher). **Advance**
  gives money in advance, or records money returned.
- **Give advance**, and **Add expense** for company-account costs (services, licences…; no employee, bill optional)
  or on behalf of someone. Accounts' own entries count straight away.
- **All expenses** with filters (person / company, status, dates) and **PDF / Excel**. The dashboard's Finance
  section shows approved **Expenses** for the period.

## 12. Dashboard

- **Top strip** (today's position): active leads → opportunities → open quotations → open sales orders → clients →
  unpaid invoices → to-dos due. Tap any number to see the records.
- **Show:** All · Sales · Finance · Team & management · Service & delivery (pick one or several; remembered).
- **Period:** This month · Last month · This quarter · This financial year · Last 30 days · Custom; salesperson and
  state filters for managers. In a past year the dashboard opens on the whole year.
- Salespeople see only their own numbers.

## 13. Year end and renewals

- On 1 April just choose the new year (e.g. **2027-28**) after login. Open leads, deals and unpaid invoices carry
  over; nothing is deleted.
- Receipt numbers (117/26-27) and PO numbers (PO/2627/93) restart each financial year.
- **Renewals** are for the **next financial year**: Clients → **Create renewals** (or **Renewal FY …** on one
  client). Each gets a "Renewal for FY 2027-28" opportunity valued at this year's orders, due to close by 31 March,
  with a call in 7 days.
- Clients → **In 2026-27: Not renewed** lists who hasn't ordered again.

## 17. Common questions

- **Where did my lead go?** Check the year at the top, and the status filter (it starts on **All**). A converted
  lead lives on as an opportunity.
- **I typed the mobile / name wrong.** Fix it anywhere: the lead page, **Edit school details** in the opportunity,
  or **Edit details** on the client. All of them update together.
- **Why can't I see a colleague's school?** Salespeople see only their own. Ask an Admin to reassign it.
- **The school wants fewer items / extra items.** Quotation → **Change kit items**. The PO lists what's excluded
  and the checklist shows the final kit.
- **Transport differs by city.** Type it in **Transport / kit (hidden)** on the quotation line. It's inside the
  price and never shown.
- **A school paid by cheque.** Record payment with mode Cheque / PDC; mark **Deposited**, then **Cleared** when the
  bank confirms (only then does it count as received).
- **A school didn't renew.** Nothing is lost. Clients → **In 2026-27: Not renewed** → call them; their full history
  is on their page.
